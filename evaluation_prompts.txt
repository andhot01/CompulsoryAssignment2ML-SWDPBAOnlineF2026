10 test prompts to evaluate agent

1: "Find a paper about artificial intelligence agents with at least 100 citations. Explain why it is relevant."
What the prompt tests - a broad topic with minimum citation constraint and relevance explanation.

2: "Find a paper about LLM agents for software engineering published after 2021 with at least 100 citations. Explain why the paper is relevant and provide the source of the
citation count."
What the prompt tests - a narrow topic with an after-year constraint, a citation minimum and citation source requirement.

3: "Find a paper about retrieval-augmented generation published before 2021 with more than 500 citations. Summarize its contribution in 5-7 sentences."
What the prompt tests - before-year constraint with a citation minimum and a summary request.

4: "Find a paper about tool-using AI agents published in 2023 with at least 50 citations. Explain whether it would be useful for someone building autonomous software agents."
What the prompt tests - narrow topic with exact year constraint and explanation request.

5: "Find a paper about natural language processing published in 2020 with at least 500 citations. Give a short explanation of why it matches."
What the prompt tests - broad topic with an exact year constraint, high citation count and explanation request.

6: "Find a recent paper about AI agents using tools and explain whether it would be useful for building autonomous software agents."
What the prompt tests - ambiguos request (recent - meaning max 2-3 years ago).

7: "Find a paper about retrieval-augmented generation published after 2022 with fewer than 200 citations. Explain why it matches the request."
What the prompt tests - maximum citation constraint.

8: "Find a paper about AI agents with approximately 100 citations published after 2021. Explain any uncertainty in the match."
What the prompt tests - approximation (should be plus minus 20%)

9: "Find a paper about retrieval-augmented generation published before 2008 with more than 5000 citations. Summarize its contribution."
What the prompt tests - probabaæy no valid result, potential invented answer or hallucination.

10: "Find an important paper about AI systmes and explain why it matters."
What the prompt tests - ambiguous and subjective topic, underspecified request.
